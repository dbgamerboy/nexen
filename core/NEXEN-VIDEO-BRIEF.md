## Claims and measurement rules

- **Code completion:** unmeasurable as a single percentage because “all plans and all features” has no fixed, fully enumerated denominator. Do not infer a percentage from file counts, lines of code, image coverage or generated prompts.
- **Task completion:** the Money Engine's percentage is `completed matching tasks / all matching tasks × 100`. Its current scope is tracked tasks mentioning Lumipaw, dropshipping, music release or WDR brand. Completion is locally recorded, not proof of payment, campaign execution or provider success. If showing a number, capture the live numerator, denominator and scope together; do not insert an example percentage.
- **Image review:** the current catalogue contains 665 source files grouped into 347 unique assets, including 318 additional duplicate copies. Six unique assets were visually inspected; 341 remain pending. This is visual-review coverage, not code or business completion. Refresh the catalogue before capture and revise these figures if the verified records changed.
- **Prompt packets:** `/plans` stores prepared, traceable implementation packages. Preparing or compiling them does not run code or spend money. Export coverage remains incomplete; do not say every conversation or plan has been fully consolidated.
- **Money:** actual revenue and ad spending are **unknown**, not zero. Their connectors are absent, and paid ad execution is disabled in the Money Engine. The authorized Lumipaw ceilings are $50 total and $50 per day, with no automatic restart, but platform enforcement is not verified. The video must not imply an active campaign or guaranteed income.
- **Connections:** “installed,” “configured,” “reachable” and “logged in” are different states. Show the exact verified state. Independent local work can continue while a connected action waits for login or readiness verification.
- **Game and lookbook:** existing movement/driving and workspace access are demonstrable. Clothing boards remain references; do not imply that all garments are modeled, wearable in the game or ready for manufacture.
