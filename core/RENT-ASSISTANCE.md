## Short call script

“Hi, I live in ZIP 97233. My rent is due September 21, and I am $1,600 short. I want to prevent losing my housing. I [have / have not] received a written rent or eviction notice. Are you accepting applications for a program that could fit my situation? What is the earliest screening appointment, and what documents do you need?”

Then ask:

1. “Does this program help before rent is overdue, or does it require arrears or a notice?”
2. “Can you screen my household income and lease situation? Is a referral required?”
3. “If intake is full, exactly when should I call again, and is there another current provider?”
4. “What is my reference number, next deadline and best follow-up contact?”

Describe only facts that are true. A partial pledge, referral or pending application is not payment approval.

## Prepare and follow through

- Gather the signed lease, current rent ledger/balance, any notice, and recent household income records. Confirm each agency's required list and secure submission method before sending personal documents. SEI's current instructions include 30 days of income, lease pages and applicable notices. [SEI documents](https://www.selfenhancement.org/housing-assistance)
- Record the time, person or voicemail, program name, appointment, missing documents and next callback date in `resources.json` or a private note.
- Ask the landlord/property manager for a written balance and discuss a realistic written arrangement while applications are pending. Do not promise an agency payment before it is confirmed.
- If a notice or court paper exists, tell 211 immediately and ask for legal-service referral as well as rent help. The county directs people facing eviction to both forms of support. [County referral guidance](https://multco.us/info/how-access-emergency-rent-assistance)
- Track actual written commitments and actual payments separately. Tell providers about other pending assistance for the same balance so funds can be coordinated.
- If 211's weekly referrals are closed, note the next business-week retry. For St. Vincent de Paul, leave only one application voicemail during the permitted window; its published response range is two days to two weeks. [County capacity](https://multco.us/info/how-access-emergency-rent-assistance), [SVdP process](https://www.svdppdx.org/applications-open-notice)

The state's current housing-help directory remains a fallback for checking routes; historical pandemic-era OERAP forms are not included as current applications. [Oregon housing resources](https://www.oregon.gov/ohcs/Pages/housing-resources.aspx)

## If a termination notice or court papers exist

Oregon Law Center's Eviction Defense Project: **888-585-9638** (TextNow: `8885859638`). It screens low-income tenants facing eviction; some Multnomah tenants can receive help after a termination notice and before court. Contact promptly, before the first court appearance, with the real case number and hearing date if there is one. Leave one message; high demand means a response or representation is not guaranteed. Intake hours are not published on the current page. EDP does not supply rent money, rental referrals or application help. [Official EDP instructions](https://oregonlawcenter.org/eviction-defense-project/)

The Portland-funded service also covers qualifying termination notices or subsidy loss, requires a rental unit within Portland city limits and income at or below 60% AMI. ZIP 97233 does not by itself verify the city boundary. [City eligibility and contact](https://www.portland.gov/phb/rental-services/eviction-help-renters/eviction-legal-support-tenants)
